Quanzhou (泉州) in Fujian, China, is a historical city that I happen to have several friends from there. I randomly chose to relax there. Without hesitation, I booked the hotel and transport one week before departure.

I brought four rolls of 135 colour negative films, my film SLR and three lenses with me. Yes, there is no digital camera. I sent the negatives to the New Color (新彩) studio in Guangzhou for development and digitisation (scanning). I later processed the TIFF files with Nikon's NX Studio software.

The photos were taken from 2 to 5 July 2024, and the film rolls were developed on 7 July.

Picture were shot on these films:
- Fujifilm Superia Premium 400
- Kodak Professional Ektar 100
- Kodak Professional ProImage 100
- Kodak Portra 400
