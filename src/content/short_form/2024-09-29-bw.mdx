I love black and white (B&W) photography a lot. It makes me think simple and pure. The elimination of the colour forces me to think about the composition, subjects, and the story I would like to tell.

By now, most of my B&W photos have been taken in film and slightly adjusted with Photoshop.

Film rolls include
- Kodak EASTMAN DOUBLE-X 5222
- Ilford XP2 Super 400
- Ilford HP5 Plus
