## About me

I am a postdoctoral fellow in [The Department of Imaging and Interventional Radiology](http://www.diir.cuhk.edu.hk/), [Faculty of Medicine](https://www.med.cuhk.edu.hk/), [The Chinese University of Hong Kong](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/), working on multi-modality deep learning methods and their application on MRI and Nasopharyngeal Carcinoma (NPC).

Before that, I finished my PhD training in the same department with [Prof. Weitian Chen](https://www.diir.cuhk.edu.hk/staff/weitian-chen/) in 2025, focused on application of deep learning in MRI and the detailed topics includes deep learning-based reconstruction, quantification, and diagnostics with 3D and quantitative MRI.

My early career began at [The Department of Computer Science and Technology](https://fst.uic.edu.cn/cst/), [Faculty of Science and Technology](https://fst.uic.edu.cn/), [Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University](https://uic.edu.cn) (formerly known as BNU-HKBU United International College). I got my Bachelor of Science degree there in 2018, and worked as an assistant instructor there from 2018 to 2019. I later got my Master of Science degree in 2020 at [The Department of Information Engineering](https://www.ie.cuhk.edu.hk/), [Faculty of Engineering](https://www.erg.cuhk.edu.hk/erg/), The Chinese University of Hong Kong.
